The Awkward Silence Timer 🐐

Match two people. If they go quiet past the threshold, Ex-Files calls both of them and plays the same goat scream (Africa's Talking Voice). Conversation resumes out of confusion. Run the server with SILENCE_MS=8000 to watch it fire in 8 seconds.

The Talking-Stage Clock ⏳

Ex-Files' job is to end the talking stage. Every match has a shelf life: at the halfway mark the app auto-proposes a coffee date ☕. Set a plan before the deadline or the match expires — no more three-week chats that go nowhere.
